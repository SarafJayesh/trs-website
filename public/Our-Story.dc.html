OUR STORY

Built from a promise. Served with care since 2016.

In 1990, Leela Gupta's husband opened a restaurant in Mount Abu. After his passing in 1993, she held on to his dream of running a pure vegetarian kitchen of their own. In 2016, that promise became The Rasoiya Street — and every plate since has been held to it.

THE NAME

Many choices, served with one standard of care.

Rasoiya is the person who feeds with care, responsibility and memory — that's trust. Street is many kitchens and cravings side by side — that's variety. The Rasoiya Street is both: a place built to help a mixed family agree on one table, with something for every generation.

What we stand for

Trust

The meal feels safe, consistent, pure veg and cared for — every time, at every outlet.

Variety

A broad menu organised around family choice, so everyone finds something they want.

Elevation

A step above value dining — cleaner interiors, better plating, warmer service.
