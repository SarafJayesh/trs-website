SECTION 01
Brand Essence
The Rasoiya Street is not just a broad vegetarian menu. Its job is to help a mixed family agree on one table with confidence, care, and enough variety for every craving.
POSITION  For vegetarian families who want everyone at the table to agree, The Rasoiya Street is the elevated pure-veg family table where every generation finds something they love - done right, every time.
The Real Tension
The tension is not price. It is agreement. Grandparents, parents, kids, and groups need one place where everyone feels considered and no one worries the food will not be right.

SECTION 02
Story and Role
The founder story gives trust its proof. Use it to show care, responsibility, and pure-veg conviction, not nostalgia for its own sake.
Founder Proof
The brand began from Leela Gupta's promise to carry forward her late husband's dream of opening a pure vegetarian restaurant. That gives the word trust emotional and operational credibility.
STORY GUARDRAIL  Use the story to prove care, commitment, and pure-veg conviction. Avoid 'homely mother food'; it pulls the brand into value territory. Use the story sparingly and tastefully.

SECTION 03
Verbal Identity
The voice is warm, confident, family-first, and specific. Lead with one clear reason to visit; prove it with detail, not adjectives.
LEAD LINE  Every cuisine. One table you can trust.
Standing brand anchor: The Rasoiya Street | Pure-Veg Dining | Since 2016
Voice Principles
Warm: speak like a host who understands families, not a template caption.
Confident: make pure veg feel premium and easy, not defensive.
Specific: say what to order, why it works, and what moment it suits.
Family-first: solve the agreement problem without sounding old-fashioned.

Locked Direction
Element

Designer to specify
Logo
Simplify the kadai / rasoi symbol. Keep the orange + teal memory. Remove badge clutter, curved taglines, legal marks, and cartoon typography from the primary lockup.
Final mark; horizontal, stacked, icon-only, reversed variants; clear-space and min-size.
Palette
Warm orange, clean teal, deep charcoal, cream/off-white, and small fresh accents. Avoid a one-note orange theme or dark luxury palette.
HEX, CMYK, Pantone, primary/secondary ratios, accessibility checks.
Typography
Rounded but refined. Clear, confident, friendly. No comic, distorted, or overly playful lettering for brand use.
Headline and body typefaces, type scale, web/print fallbacks.
Graphic motifs
Simple vessel, table, spice, or signage cues used sparingly. Avoid dense motifs that read as wedding or banquet.
Approved motif set and usage rules.
Layout
Clean hierarchy, generous spacing, strong food photography, and proof-led copy.
Grid, spacing tokens, and template files.

Application Principles
Every channel should make the positioning visible: pure veg without anxiety, variety without chaos, and a lifted family-dining experience without fine-dining stiffness.
Channel Rules

Principle
Social media

Lead with the brand role, then prove it: hero, proof bar, variety by use-case, founder promise, outlet finder, gallery, reviews.

Guideline

Mark 8-12 must-try items. Do not make every item special.

Pricing
Easy to scan. Mid-to-premium and repeatable; not a bargain sheet or fine-dine manifesto.
